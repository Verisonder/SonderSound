# SonderSound

Tells you when someone calls your name while you wear earphones.

- Teach it a sound — your name, a doorbell, anything — and it chimes in your earphones when it hears it.
- Hear back the last 15 or 30 seconds.
- Optionally transcribe them with your own Gemini key.

Nothing leaves the phone unless you ask for a transcript.
